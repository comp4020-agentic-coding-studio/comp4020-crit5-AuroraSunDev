1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

   The most important breakthrough in Crit 5 was the Stranger Test. After I asked Claude to build the project based on my ideas, I tested it myself and felt that it met every requirement in the Crit 5 spec. Then I invited a friend to try it locally, and I found several problems that I had not noticed.

   For example, my friend did not know how to play when first entering the game. The collision area of the upper and lower obstacles was also larger than what it looked like visually, which made the game feel unfair and frustrating. These were problems that only became clear through stranger testing.

   I improved the game based on my friend's feedback, and this made a big difference to the overall player experience.

2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

   I think a good software developer should not rely only on their own ideas. They also need other people's perspectives.

   This is especially important when using AI for vibe coding, because it is easy for a project to become rigid or feel too machine-made. As a software developer, I should not only pay attention to the details of the code, but also to whether the final result actually works well for real users.

   In other words, the experience of an ordinary user is something AI may not fully consider on its own.
